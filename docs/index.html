Scheduled supply vs. population, by commune

Metric: scheduled stop-visits per 1,000 residents on a representative weekday (every scheduled bus/tram passage at a stop counts as one visit).

Population is a proxy for demand, not real ridership. Stops are attributed to the nearest commune center, not to real commune boundaries — Le Mans neighborhoods near a suburb (e.g. the University campus) are counted in that suburb, which inflates small neighboring communes and deflates Le Mans itself.
